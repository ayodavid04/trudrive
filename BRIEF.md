## The three directions

### A. Solid (grammar: split stage)

Before on one side, after on the other, for the whole page. The divider is the chrome and carries the
phone number. The visitor can drag it. The close is the collapse: the after side lays itself across the
before side slab by slab until one finished patio fills the screen, and the quote sits in it.

Feeling curve:
1. Recognition: their own tired garden on one side, a finished one on the other, both readable at once.
2. Trust: each job shown both ways, and they can drag the divider themselves.
3. Relief: their problem in their words on the before side, answered plainly on the after side.
4. Reassurance: how a quote works and where reviews go, one per side.
5. Satisfaction (peak): the after side paves over the before side, slab by slab.
6. Resolve: one finished screen, the quote, held.

Peak: "the old decking got paved over in front of me, one slab at a time, then the whole screen was the new patio." Act 5.
It's the site where you watch the new patio get laid over the old decking, slab by slab.

### B. Premium (grammar: gallery / catalog)

His jobs as a collection with gallery labels. Facts, not pitch. Deep navy from the logo, bone type, one
sandstone accent. The nav is an index of the jobs and it jumps.

Feeling curve:
1. Calm: one finished garden, large, labelled like a piece in a gallery.
2. (authored silence) a short quiet label-only beat.
3. Satisfaction (peak): the second piece lays itself slab by slab over the rotten decking it replaced.
4. Breadth: the rest of the collection travels sideways, each one draggable before and after.
5. Understanding: the materials, as plain labels.
6. Trust: reviews, set as a plate.
7. Intent: the enquiry plate, typeset like a label.

Peak: "a photo of rotten decking turned into a patio one slab at a time while I scrolled." Act 3.
It's the site that feels like a gallery of his jobs, and one of them lays itself while you scroll.

### C. Bold (grammar: rhythmic cutlist)

His TikTok voice and his flyer colours. Hard cuts, loud bar with the phone number at wordmark weight,
nothing pinned. The peak runs as the section passes: slabs slam down across the screen.

Feeling curve:
1. Pulse: FROM THIS, over the worst before photo.
2. Punch: TO THIS, the after.
3. Pace: what he lays, one word a cut.
4. Thrill (peak): LAID, the patio slams down slab by slab as it passes.
5. Play: drag the jobs yourself.
6. Pride: 15 years, the North West.
7. Proof: reviews.
8. Decision: full bleed blue, get a free quote.

Peak: "the patio slammed down slab by slab as I scrolled past it." Act 4.
It's the site that talks like his TikTok: from this, to this, then the patio slams down slab by slab.

## Signature move (all three, executed differently)

Slab laying: the after photo is cut into slab-shaped tiles in stretcher bond and laid over the before
photo in laying order, row by row from the far end, each tile lowered into place with its shadow, open
joints while laying, grouted closed at the end so the final frame is exactly his photo. Canvas, driven by
scroll progress. Reduced motion gets a plain crossfade.
