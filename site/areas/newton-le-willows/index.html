Do you cover Newton-le-Willows?

Yes. Newton-le-Willows is about 6 miles from our base in Wigan, and we cover Newton-le-Willows and Earlestown (WA12). Ring Gaz on 07923 485811 or send a few photos on WhatsApp.

Do I need a dropped kerb in Newton-le-Willows?

Yes, if you'll drive over the pavement to get onto the drive. In Newton-le-Willows you apply to St Helens Council. They assess it and send a free quote, and the crossing can be built by the council or by a contractor. On an A, B or C road you may need planning permission first. It can take a while, so it's worth applying early. More on St Helens Council's dropped kerb page.

Do I need planning permission for a new drive in Newton-le-Willows?

Not usually. A new front drive doesn't need planning permission if the surface lets water through, or the rain drains into a lawn or border on your own land. If it's over 5 square metres, solid and drains onto the road, it does, and in Newton-le-Willows you'd apply to St Helens Council.

How much does a new driveway or patio cost in Newton-le-Willows?

Every job is priced on its size, the material and what has to come up first. Send a few photos on WhatsApp to 07923 485811 and Gaz will give you a free price. If it's worth a visit, he'll come out and measure up.
