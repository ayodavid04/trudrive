Questions from Chorley

Do you cover Chorley?

Yes. Chorley is about 7 miles from our base in Wigan, and we cover Chorley, Adlington, Coppull and Euxton (PR6 and PR7). Ring Gaz on 07923 485811 or send a few photos on WhatsApp.

Do I need a dropped kerb in Chorley?

Yes, if you'll drive over the pavement to get onto the drive. Chorley is in Lancashire, so it goes through Lancashire County Council rather than the local borough council. You apply online, and if it's approved they send you a list of approved contractors to get prices from. On an A, B or C road you'll need planning permission first. More on Lancashire County Council's dropped kerb page.

Do I need planning permission for a new drive in Chorley?

Not usually. A new front drive doesn't need planning permission if the surface lets water through, or the rain drains into a lawn or border on your own land. If it's over 5 square metres, solid and drains onto the road, it does, and in Chorley you'd apply to Chorley Council.
