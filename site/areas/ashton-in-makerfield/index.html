Do I need a dropped kerb in Ashton-in-Makerfield?

Yes, if you'll drive over the pavement to get onto the drive, and in Ashton-in-Makerfield that goes through Wigan Council. You apply with their vehicle crossing form and they reply within 20 working days. If it's approved, they take a deposit and build the crossing themselves. On an A, B or C road you need planning permission first, and council tenants need written permission from their estates manager. More on Wigan Council's dropped kerb page.

Do I need planning permission for a new drive in Ashton-in-Makerfield?

Not usually. A new front drive doesn't need planning permission if the surface lets water through, or the rain drains into a lawn or border on your own land. If it's over 5 square metres, solid and drains onto the road, it does, and in Ashton-in-Makerfield you'd apply to Wigan Council.
