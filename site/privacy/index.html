Privacy

The short version: this website doesn't set cookies or track you, and we only use your details to quote for and do your job.

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, no tracking or analytics, and no accounts.

The quote form

The form doesn't send anything to us through this website. When you tap Send, it opens WhatsApp on your phone with your message filled in. Nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy covers the message.

When you get in touch

If you ring, text or message us, we use your name, number, address and any photos you send to give you a quote and do the job. We keep them only as long as we need them for your quote, the job and our records. We don't sell your details or use them for marketing.

Services this website uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the website. Like any web host, it handles your IP address to send you the pages and keep the site secure.
  • Fontshare supplies the lettering. Your browser fetches it from their servers, which sees your IP address.
  • TikTok: a video marked TikTok on our work page plays in TikTok's own player. Nothing loads from TikTok until you press play; once you do, TikTok's privacy policy covers the player, including any cookies it sets.
  • Links to WhatsApp, TikTok and Google take you to their sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Get in touch using the number above. If you're not happy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
