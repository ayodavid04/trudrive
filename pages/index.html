Four looks for the TruDrive website

Same photos, same features in each. What changes is the feel. Have a scroll through them on your phone, then say which one feels most like TruDrive. We can mix bits from different ones too.

  1. Our pick

    Clean

    A proper website with all the pages: home, services, our work, about, reviews and a free quote page. Bright and simple, your photos front and centre, one before and after slide on the home page. Built to be what your customers expect, done better than the firms round you.

    Open Clean

  2. Solid

    Plain and honest. The whole site is before on one side, after on the other, and you can drag the line between them. At the end the new patio lays itself over the old garden.

    Open Solid

  3. Premium

    Quiet and high end, like a gallery of your jobs. Navy from your logo, lots of space, each job labelled with what was laid. One job lays itself slab by slab as you scroll.

    Open Premium

  4. Bold

    Loud, like your TikTok and your flyer. Blue, white and black, big letters, your number at the top the whole time. From this, to this, then the slabs slam down.

    Open Bold

In all four

  • Your own photos. These are from your TikTok for now. Your originals will look sharper.
  • Quotes come to your WhatsApp. The customer fills in what they need and it opens WhatsApp to you, ready for them to add photos. Nothing to log into.
  • Your Google reviews show up by themselves. After a job you text the customer one link.
  • Your number on every screen, one tap to ring.
  • Before and afters on the jobs where there's a before photo.
