Questions people ask

Do you cover my town?

We're based in Wigan, so anywhere in the Wigan borough, and the towns around it listed above. If you're further out in Greater Manchester, Merseyside, Lancashire, Cheshire or Cumbria, send a message on WhatsApp or ring {{phone}} and Gaz will tell you straight away.

What do you lay?

Driveways in block paving, imprinted concrete, tarmac and gravel. Patios in porcelain, Indian sandstone and crazy paving. Also paths and steps, fencing, brickwork and turfing.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Send a few photos of your drive or garden and Gaz comes out, measures up and gives you a price. There's no obligation.

How long have you been doing this?

Over 15 years laying driveways and patios across the North West.
