Why imprinted concrete

No joints, no weeds

Because it's one slab, there are no gaps between blocks for weeds or moss to grow in, and a sweep keeps it clean.

Patterns and colours

The concrete is stamped while it's wet with a pattern, like brick, cobble or slate, and coloured to suit the house. A border in a different pattern or colour frames it.

Looking after it

It's sealed when it's laid, which brings out the colour and protects the surface. A fresh coat of sealer every few years keeps it looking new.

Questions about imprinted concrete

Does an imprinted concrete drive need planning permission?

Imprinted concrete doesn't let water through, so a front drive over 5 square metres needs the rain to run off into a lawn, a border or a drain on your own land. If it drains onto the road, it needs planning permission. A drainage channel that takes the water into your garden or a soakaway is the usual way round it.

How do you keep imprinted concrete looking good?

Give it a sweep and a wash now and then, and have it resealed every few years. The sealer brings the colour back and protects the surface.

Imprinted concrete or block paving?

Imprinted concrete if you want no joints and no weeds. Block paving if you want the classic look and the option of lifting a single block to repair it.
